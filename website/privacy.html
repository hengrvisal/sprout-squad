Privacy

Last updated 29 September 2026

Sprout Squad is a small app for logging what you get done each day and sharing that with friends you choose. This page explains, in plain English, what we store and who can see it.

What we collect

WhatWhy
Your email addressTo send your sign-in code. It isn’t shown to other people.
Display name and emoji avatarSo your squad knows it’s you.
What you log: the text, its category and the dateThis is the app: your grid, streak and today’s list.
Squads you join and kudos you send or receiveSo squadmates can see each other and cheer each other on.
Waitlist email and phone type (website form)To invite you to the beta. Nothing else.

We don’t collect your location, contacts, photos or advertising identifiers, and the app has no analytics or tracking SDKs.

Who can see it

  • People in a squad with you can see your name, avatar, grid, streak, what you logged and kudos. Nobody else can.
  • There are no public profiles and no feed for strangers.
  • If you leave a squad, its members can no longer see your data (unless you share another squad with them).

Services we use

  • Supabase hosts the database and handles sign-in.
  • Resend delivers the sign-in code emails.
  • Apple distributes the beta through TestFlight, under Apple’s own privacy policy.

We don’t sell your data or share it with advertisers.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data while you have an account. You can delete your account at any time in the app (Me → Delete account), which permanently removes your profile, everything you’ve logged, your kudos and your squad memberships straight away. See Delete your account.

To be removed from the beta waitlist, email hello@sproutsquad.app.

Children

Sprout Squad isn’t intended for children under 13.

Changes

If we change what we collect or who can see it, we’ll update this page and the date at the top, and tell you in the app for anything significant.

Contact

Questions or requests about your data: hello@sproutsquad.app
