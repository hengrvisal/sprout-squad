How this came to be

Most productivity apps want you to optimise. Set goals, hit targets, beat your streak, beat your friends. It’s a lot of pressure for something that should feel good.

We wanted something smaller. Write down what you actually did today, even if it was just the laundry. Let a few people who care about you see it. Notice that you’ve been showing up more than you thought.

Good to know

Is it free?

Yes. The beta is free, and there are no ads.

Which phones does it work on?

The beta is on iPhone, through Apple’s TestFlight app. Android is planned. Pick “Android” when you join and we’ll let you know.

Who can see what I log?

Only people in a squad with you. There’s no public profile and no feed for strangers. If you’re not in a squad, it’s just you.

Who can see my plans and notes?

Your plan for the day is visible to your squad, like your wins. Kudos notes are private: only you and the person you wrote to can read them.

Will it spam me with notifications?

No. You get an evening check-in only if you haven’t logged anything that day, one heads-up per friend per day when they cheer you on, and a quiet-friend nudge at most once per quiet stretch. Each one has its own switch in the app.

Can I delete my data?

Any time, from inside the app: Me → Delete account. It removes everything straight away. More on deleting your account.

What happens after I join the beta?

We’ll email you a TestFlight invite when there’s a spot. Install TestFlight, tap the link, and you’re in.
